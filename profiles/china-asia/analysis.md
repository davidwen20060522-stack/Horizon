# Evaluation goal

Evaluate whether a China/Asia item gives readers timely, credible understanding of policy, economy, technology, or regional dynamics whose significance depends on the regional context.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Systemic.** National-level policy shifts (fiscal, monetary, regulatory, industrial), major cross-strait or regional-security developments, or structural changes affecting large populations or whole sectors.
- **7-8: Important.** Concrete stimulus or regulation with figures and scope, significant platform-company restructuring, meaningful trade or supply-chain realignment involving Asia, notable diplomatic moves with stated outcomes.
- **5-6: Useful.** Credible regional developments with specifics but limited reach, provincial policy with clear sector effect, company moves tied to Asia strategy.
- **3-4: Low value.** Symbolic diplomacy without decisions, local events detached from regional significance, recycled announcements.
- **0-2: Noise.** Propaganda without verifiable facts, rumor aggregates, lifestyle consumption stories without analytical value.

# Evaluation guidance

Weigh whether the item has concrete policy instruments, figures, or named decisions rather than slogans or attendance-only reporting. Consider second-order effects on markets, industries, and neighboring economies. Treat state-media framing cautiously and reward independently verifiable specifics.

Use three to five specific topic tags.

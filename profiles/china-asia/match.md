# China & Asia profile

Use this profile when an item's primary subject is mainland China, Hong Kong, Taiwan, or the broader Asia-Pacific region, and its value depends on that regional context.

Typical items include Chinese macro policy and stimulus, PBOC and regulatory decisions, mainland tech-platform regulation and platform strategy, cross-strait and China-Japan-Korea economic ties, ASEAN and India industrial and supply-chain shifts, regional security diplomacy centered on Asia, and Asia-Pacific market and energy developments.

Route items where China appears only as one name among many global actors to the world politics or finance profile. Do not route intra-China lifestyle news, provincial propaganda, or region-irrelevant company PR here.

# Evaluation goal

Evaluate whether a business news item reveals a concrete, consequential change in how a company or industry operates, competes, or creates value.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Structural.** Industry-redefining M&A, landmark antitrust or breakup outcomes, funding/IPO events that reset a sector's economics, or strategic pivots with demonstrated large-scale consequences.
- **7-8: Important.** Material deals, funding rounds, executive changes at major firms, supply-chain reallocations, or business consequences of regulation, with named figures or verifiable scope.
- **5-6: Useful.** Credible specific developments about a real company or market segment that inform understanding but with limited breadth.
- **3-4: Low value.** Routine store openings, minor partnerships, unsourced executive quotes, funding items with undisclosed terms.
- **0-2: Noise.** Promotional PR, founder hype, listicles, speculation presented as fact.

# Evaluation guidance

Reward verifiable transactions, named amounts, and concrete operational change. Distinguish announced intent from closed deals. Penalize items whose significance rests only on brand fame.

Use three to five specific topic tags.

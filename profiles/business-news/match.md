# Business news profile

Use this profile when an item's primary value is corporate and industry dynamics rather than market prices or technology itself.

Typical items include company strategy shifts, mergers, acquisitions and divestitures, startup funding rounds, IPOs, leadership changes, large layoffs or hiring programs, supply-chain restructuring, major product-business decisions, retail and consumer trends with concrete data, antitrust actions against companies, and industry-level regulation with clear business consequences.

Route pure macro data, interest rates, inflation, and market-moving financial statistics to the finance profile. Route product launches, model releases, and engineering milestones to the technology profile. Do not route press-release-style promotion, founder interviews without new facts, or listicles here.

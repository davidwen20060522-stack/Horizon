# World Politics profile

Use this profile when an item's primary value is government, diplomacy, conflict, or institutions at the international or national level, beyond a purely economic or regional frame.

Typical items include wars and ceasefires, sanctions and their enforcement, elections with national direction consequences, NATO/EU/UN institutional decisions, major treaties and diplomatic realignments, migration and border policy, political crises, and significant court rulings on governmental power.

Route items whose decisive consequence is market-wide economics to the finance profile, corporate regulation to business, and Asia-centered state affairs to the China & Asia profile. Do not route routine speeches without decisions, polls without stakes, or local crime stories framed as politics here.

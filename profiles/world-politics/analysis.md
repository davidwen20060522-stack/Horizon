# Evaluation goal

Evaluate whether a political or diplomatic item reflects a real decision or development with consequences beyond rhetoric.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Systemic.** Outbreak or major change of war, ceasefire or treaty with binding terms, sweeping sanctions packages, institutional realignment (NATO/EU/UN), or constitutional-level domestic change with international effect.
- **7-8: Important.** Election outcomes with direction consequences, significant sanctions enforcement or diplomatic breakthroughs, major legislation on migration/security, cabinet-level policy reversals.
- **5-6: Useful.** Concrete but narrow developments: a specific vote, a named appointment with stake, a regional agreement with substance.
- **3-4: Low value.** Speeches without decisions, polls, diplomatic meetings with stated outcomes only.
- **0-2: Noise.** Speculation, personality-driven commentary, unverified battlefield or scandal claims presented as fact.

# Evaluation guidance

Reward signed, passed, enacted, or otherwise verifiable actions. Separate positions from outcomes. Consider breadth: affected populations, alliance structure, security architecture. Do not reward emotional framing or urgency language without substance.

Use three to five specific topic tags.
